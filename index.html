CAMPUSCARE SITE GUIDE

Help using CampusCare

Quick answers about signing in and using the website.

01
Sign in to your account

Choose Student or Staff, enter the email or ID for that account, then select Sign in. Demo accounts can fill the fields for you.

02
Find your reports

Open My complaints to search and filter reports. Select a report to see its details, updates, and assigned technician information.

03
Check updates and messages

The bell shows when a technician is assigned, a visit is scheduled, or a report changes. Open Updates for the latest status and visit information.

04
Manage your account

Select your profile in the top bar to view account details or sign out.

05
Confirm the repair

When the technician marks a report resolved, review the completion details and confirm closure. If it is not fixed, reopen or escalate it to the warden with a reason. You can also rate the service.

This prototype uses demo identities and a local SQLite database when run with server.js. It is not connected to LPU identity or maintenance systems.
